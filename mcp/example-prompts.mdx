---
title: "Example Prompts"
description: "Prompts that work well with the Parsec MCP server for research, trading, builders, and live feeds."
---

## Market research

- "What prediction markets are available about Bitcoin today?"
- "Find the most liquid election markets on Kalshi and Polymarket."
- "Show me the orderbook and recent trades for the top Trump 2028 market."
- "What same-market matches exist for this Kalshi contract?"

## Trading

- "Am I set up to trade on Polymarket?"
- "Preview buying 50 Yes contracts on the top Bitcoin market."
- "Place a small order on that market only if the preview shows enough liquidity."
- "List my open orders and tell me which ones are still working."

## Builders

- "Create a new end-user for external_id user_001 and show me the customer_id."
- "Onboard that user to Polymarket with managed mode."
- "Check whether fee escrow is enabled for my builder account."
- "List my users and show which exchanges each one is linked to."

## Live monitoring

- "Open a live feed for the top ETH market and summarize the next 20 messages."
- "Watch this market and tell me when the best ask drops below 0.45."
- "Open a feed for both sides of this market and summarize book changes."

## Good prompt habits

- mention the exchange if you already know it
- mention `parsec_id` when you have an exact market
- ask for a preview before a live order
- ask the client to explain the final action before you approve it
